OUR STORY

About Onke's Laundry Service

Onke's Laundry Service was born in Cape Town from a simple idea: to give busy families, professionals and businesses back their time while keeping their clothes fresh, clean and beautifully cared for.

We started small — washing for neighbours and friends — and grew through word-of-mouth because we treat every item like our own. From everyday wear to delicate fabrics, bedding, curtains and business uniforms, we use quality, eco-friendly products and hygienic processes.

Today we serve homes and businesses across Cape Town and surroundings with free collection & delivery, same-day options, and affordable pricing. Our promise is simple: Reliable. Affordable. Always fresh.
